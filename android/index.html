Message Link for Android

All four services in one inbox on your phone, through your own Message Link Server.

In invite-only testing on Google Play. It isn't public yet. This page will link to the Play listing once it's up.

A look around

Pictures use made-up people.

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 12 or later.
  • A Message Link Server, on your Windows PC or a computer that's always on. Get started shows how to choose.
  • A free Tailscale account, so the phone reaches the server away from home. Message Link runs Tailscale itself, so you don't need the Tailscale app.
  • Notifications set up on the server with a free Firebase project of your own. A phone needs them before it can join.

Join your server

  1. Show the code. On the server's page, choose Add a phone.

  2. Scan it. In Message Link on the phone, choose I have a server, then Scan your server's code. There's nothing to type.

  3. Sign in to Tailscale with the same account as the server, if the phone asks.

  4. Check the server shown, then choose Join.

Want to see it first? Look around with sample conversations on the welcome screen shows Message Link with made-up people. Nothing leaves the phone.

Away from the app

  • Notifications show who wrote each message, and you can reply from them, or from a watch.
  • In the car, Android Auto lists your newest conversations, reads them aloud and takes your reply.
