Overview

All your messaging apps in one place

Google Messages, Messenger, Google Voice and Instagram, together on your PC and your phone.

Message Link isn't public yet. The guides below show how setup goes; downloads come with the first release.

Message Link is a native app for Windows 11, and there is an Android app too. It talks to each service directly, the same way the service's own app or website does. Nothing goes through anyone else's cloud: your accounts are signed in on a Message Link Server you run yourself, on your PC or at home.

What's a Message Link Server?

A small program that stays signed in to your services on a computer you own. Every PC and phone with Message Link joins one, so they all show the same conversations. Message Link for Windows has one built in.

  • It signs in to each service in its own browser. You type into the service's real sign-in page, the same as anywhere else.
  • Your phone reaches it through Tailscale, at home or away, without opening anything to the internet.
  • Phone notifications go through a Firebase project of your own. Each notification is locked so only your phone can read it.
  • It backs itself up to a folder you choose, once a day.

Message Link has no servers of its own, so your conversations and sign-ins never pass through us. Tailscale and Firebase fill that gap. Both are free, and you create your own copy of each, so they answer only to you. Only using it on a PC? You can skip both.

What each service needs

  • Google Messages: an Android phone with Google Messages. Message Link pairs with it the way Messages for Web does. An iPhone can't pair.
  • Messenger: your Facebook sign-in. Encrypted chats work too.
  • Google Voice: your Google sign-in at voice.google.com. Add as many Voice numbers as you have.
  • Instagram: your Instagram username and password. Log in with Facebook isn't supported.
