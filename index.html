Message Link

Your texts and chats from Google Messages, Messenger, Google Voice and Instagram, in one inbox.

Message Link is a native app for Windows 11, and there is an Android app too. It talks to each service directly, the same way the service's own app or website does. Nothing goes through anyone else's cloud: your accounts are signed in on your own PC, or on a Message Link Server that you run at home.

What's a Message Link Server?

A small program that stays signed in to your services on a computer you own. The Android app needs one: phones put apps to sleep, so the server keeps your accounts connected all day and tells your phone when a message arrives.

  • It signs in to each service in its own browser. You type into the service's real sign-in page, the same as anywhere else.
  • Your phone reaches it through Tailscale, at home or away, without opening anything to the internet.
  • Phone notifications go through a Firebase project of your own, sealed so Google can carry them without reading them.
  • It backs itself up to a folder you choose, once a day.

What each service needs

  • Google Messages: an Android phone with Google Messages. Message Link pairs with it the way Messages for Web does. An iPhone can't pair.
  • Messenger: your Facebook sign-in. Encrypted chats work too.
  • Google Voice: your Google sign-in at voice.google.com. Add as many Voice numbers as you have.
  • Instagram: your Instagram username and password. Log in with Facebook isn't supported.
