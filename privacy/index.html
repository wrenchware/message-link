Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Updated 2026-10-21. Applies to Message Link (Message Link for Windows and Message Link for Android) and Message Link Server.

Message Link is an app that shows your Google Messages, Messenger, Google Voice and Instagram conversations in one place. It is made by Wrenchware. This page explains what happens to your information.

The short version

  • We do not collect your data. Wrenchware has no servers that receive your messages, contacts, sign-ins or usage. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your messages stay between you and the services you already use. The app connects straight from your own device (or your own Message Link Server) to Google, Meta and Instagram, the same way their own apps and websites do.
  • Everything the app keeps is stored on your own devices, protected with Windows' or Android's own encryption.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app keeps on your device (or on a Message Link Server you run yourself):

  • your sign-ins to the services you connect, protected by your operating system so only your user account can read them;
  • copies of your conversations, contacts and photos, so they show up quickly and work offline;
  • settings and diagnostic logs. The logs hold counts, timings and outcomes. By design they leave out message text, names and phone numbers.

Nothing in this list is sent to Wrenchware. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store or Google Play version removes it; the Windows installer version asks whether to.

Who the app talks to

The app sends information only where you'd expect it to:

  • The messaging services you connect (Google Messages, Messenger, Google Voice, Instagram). Your messages, sign-ins and contacts go to them, because that is how sending and receiving works. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it.
  • Websites in links you receive, only to show a preview of the link. Links from people who aren't in your contacts are previewed only when you ask.
  • GIPHY, only if you add your own GIPHY key and search for a GIF. Your search words go to GIPHY.
  • Your own Message Link Server, if you set one up. Your phone and PC talk to it directly or over your own Tailscale network. Phone notifications from it travel encrypted through Google's Firebase service, which cannot read them.
  • Microsoft Store or Google Play, for installing and updating the app, under their own policies.

Children

Message Link is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.
